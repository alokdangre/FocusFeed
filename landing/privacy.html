Privacy Policy.

Free BYOK release. This policy describes the TypeSafe bring-your-own-key version being prepared for the Chrome Web Store. No OwnFeed account, hosted subscription, or checkout is required.

Overview

OwnFeed helps you hide YouTube content and layout elements according to your choices. Layout controls and manual blocks run locally. Optional AI topic filtering sends your chosen topics and relevant YouTube text directly to TypeSafe using your own API key.

The interactive website demo is an illustration. It does not access or filter your YouTube account. The BYOK extension does not send your filtering requests or API key to an OwnFeed-operated account or filtering server.

Information we use

On your device

The extension reads supported YouTube pages to identify video titles and IDs, channel names or IDs, descriptions, and playlist or collection information. It may fetch public channel pages to gather upload evidence for channel filtering.

Your saved topics, layout settings, manual blocks, corrections, and channel evidence remain in your browser profile. Temporary scores and diagnostics may include selected metadata and usage counts. OwnFeed does not synchronize these preferences between devices.

Your API key

The extension background worker sends your TypeSafe key only to TypeSafe over HTTPS, to check model access and authenticate AI requests. The key is held in Chrome's trusted session storage, which is not exposed to YouTube content scripts. It is not saved in browser sync, returned in diagnostics, or included in the extension package. You must re-enter it after restarting Chrome or reloading, updating, or disabling the extension. This storage choice is not protection against a compromised browser, computer, or extension.

When AI filtering is used

TypeSafe receives your chosen topics and relevant video or channel text, such as titles, channel names, description snippets, collection entries, sampled upload text, established topic labels, and a correction example when needed. Filtering requests are made directly by the background worker after you configure your key. Your use of TypeSafe is subject to its own terms, pricing, and privacy practices.

Services and sharing

  • YouTube and Google: YouTube provides the pages and public content you browse; Chrome provides extension storage. These services have their own policies.
  • TypeSafe: receives your API key for authentication and the topic/content text needed for AI matching, under your provider account.
  • Website and external links: the public website is hosted through Cloudflare. Visiting it or following GitHub and provider links involves those services and their own request handling.

The BYOK release does not use OwnFeed accounts, AWS account storage, or Dodo payments. OwnFeed does not sell your data or use filtering data for unrelated advertising or lending decisions. Data use is limited to the stated filtering and support functions; it is not transferred for unrelated purposes.

Storage and retention

Saved preferences and corrections remain in the browser profile until changed or removed. The API key is removed when you choose Remove key, restart Chrome, or reload/update/disable the extension. Temporary session diagnostics and cached decisions are cleared with the browser session; individual judgments can expire sooner.

TypeSafe's retention and handling of requests are governed by its own policy and your provider account. Removing a key or local data does not erase records already held by the provider. OwnFeed does not promise a provider retention period or training policy that it does not control.

Your choices

Change your topics, remove blocks, pause filtering, remove the API key, or uninstall OwnFeed. Layout controls and manual blocks work without a key. Removing the key stops new AI checks and invalidates cached AI decisions; saved topics and manual choices remain.

For a complete local reset, remove the extension and its local data through Chrome. Manage or revoke your key and request deletion of provider-held information directly with TypeSafe. There is no OwnFeed account to delete in this release. For privacy-related questions, use the contact route below without posting personal information.

Questions or requests

Visit OwnFeed support on GitHub ↗ for questions. For a request involving private information, ask for a private contact route without including the request details. GitHub issues are public; do not post account details, API keys, or sensitive personal information there.

We may update this policy as features and providers change. The date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
