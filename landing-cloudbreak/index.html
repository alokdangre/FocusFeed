What does OwnFeed change?

OwnFeed filters recommendations by your topics and lets you hide distracting parts of YouTube. You stay in control of what you watch.

Will it block all movie content?

No. A topic like “movie reviews” is meant to match reviews, not everything about movies. In the preview, hiding reviews keeps the video about the science behind Interstellar. You can also correct a missed video or unblock content.

Do I need an account?

Layout controls and manual channel blocking work without signing in. Hosted AI topic filtering requires an account and active Pro access. The developer preview can also connect to a locally configured backend.

Can I turn it off?

Yes. Pause filtering, change your topics, or end a Focus session whenever you like. Your preferences are yours to change.
