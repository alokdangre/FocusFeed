The preview

OwnFeed is a browser extension designed to help you shape your YouTube experience by hiding selected content, filtering recommendations, and adjusting parts of the page. Features are under development and may change, be interrupted, or produce mistakes.

The website contains an illustrative demonstration; it is not connected to your actual YouTube feed. Information about how the extension handles data is in the Privacy Policy.

Using OwnFeed

You are responsible for the topics and settings you choose and for how you use the extension. Do not use OwnFeed to interfere with other users or systems, bypass access controls, or use the service in violation of applicable law or third-party terms.

Filtering is not guaranteed to be complete or accurate. A video may be hidden or shown incorrectly, and changes to YouTube or a service provider may affect compatibility. You can change settings or stop using the extension.

Accounts and access

No OwnFeed account is needed. Layout controls and manual channel blocking work without an API key. AI topic filtering requires your own TypeSafe account, an API key, sufficient provider balance or allowance, and access to the model supported by this release.

Keep your key private, set provider usage limits, and revoke a compromised key in your TypeSafe account. The key must be re-entered after restarting Chrome or reloading/updating OwnFeed. AI filtering is an online feature and can be unavailable if the provider rejects a request or is offline.

Pricing and billing

OwnFeed is free, including its topic-filtering and Focus controls. Your TypeSafe usage is billed separately by TypeSafe under its own pricing and terms. A free extension does not include free provider usage.

OwnFeed has no paid checkout, subscription renewal, or payment refund to process in this release. Any charges, refunds, cancellations, and usage limits for your provider account are handled by TypeSafe. Future paid OwnFeed services would require their own disclosed terms before purchase.

YouTube, Google Chrome, TypeSafe, and other providers operate their own services and may apply their own terms and policies. If payment functionality is enabled later, the payment provider’s terms may also apply.

Availability and changes

The preview is provided as available. We do not promise uninterrupted service, a particular filtering result, or compatibility with every browser or YouTube layout. Features, limits, and these terms may change as the product develops; the date above will be updated when this page changes.
